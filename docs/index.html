Frequently asked questions

What is Prep4Job?

An interview-preparation app with daily questions, practical learning concepts, AI-assisted example answers and progress tracking.

Can AI-generated answers be customized?

Yes. Review each example and adapt it to your actual professional experience. Do not use invented achievements or experiences.

Does Prep4Job guarantee a job?

No. This is an educational practice tool, not a guarantee of interview or employment results.

How do I restore a Premium purchase?

Go to Account and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account originally used for the purchase.

Why don't I receive daily reminders?

Check the reminder setting in Progress and verify notification permissions for Prep4Job in iOS Settings.

How do I report a bug?

Open a support request using the link above. Include the app and iOS versions and the steps to reproduce the issue, without sharing private information.
