Privacy Policy
Política de privacidad

Last updated / Última actualización: October 23, 2026

1. About this policy

Prep4Job is an iOS app for interview preparation. This policy explains the categories of information that the app may process, how they are used, and your choices. The precise services used depend on the app version and configuration.

2. Information processed

  • Account information: if you register or sign in, the app may process your email address, display name and account identifier. Authentication credentials are handled by the authentication service.
  • Learning and practice information: completed concepts, question history, saved answers, review schedules, streaks and study activity may be saved locally on your device; signed-in users may have some progress synchronized with a backend.
  • Purchases: if you choose a paid plan, Apple and the subscription provider process transaction and entitlement information. Prep4Job does not need your full payment-card details.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the app schedules study reminders using iOS notification permissions.

3. How information is used

We use this information to provide account access, retain learning progress, display study reminders, restore or manage subscription entitlements, support app functionality, and respond to support requests.

4. Service providers

The application includes integrations for Supabase (account authentication and optional progress synchronization), RevenueCat (subscription management) and Apple's in-app purchase and notification services. Their actual use depends on the configuration of the released app.

5. AI-assisted examples

Prep4Job provides example interview answers as learning material. The reviewed app source currently uses a local demonstration answer service. If remote AI processing is enabled in a later release, this policy should be updated before launch to explain what content is shared with the provider.

6. Storage and security

Study progress may be stored on your device and some records may be synchronized when you sign in. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic service is completely secure.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings, restore purchases through the app, and request account deletion using the in-app account controls where available. Removing the app may remove locally stored data but does not necessarily cancel subscriptions or delete backend account records. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

8. Retention

Information is retained as needed to operate the app and meet applicable obligations. Data removal timing may depend on Apple or third-party service providers. We do not claim a fixed retention period that has not been verified.

9. Children

Prep4Job is intended for people preparing for employment interviews and is not designed specifically for children. Parents or guardians who have concerns may use the support channel below.

10. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, open a support request. GitHub Issues is public: do not post passwords, private answers, account identifiers or other sensitive information. We may update this policy if app functionality or service providers change.

Resumen en español

Prep4Job es una aplicación de preparación para entrevistas laborales. Si creas una cuenta, podemos tratar tu correo electrónico, nombre e identificador. La app almacena actividad de estudio, respuestas guardadas y progreso en el dispositivo; algunos datos pueden sincronizarse mediante Supabase. Apple y RevenueCat pueden intervenir en la gestión de compras y suscripciones. Los recordatorios necesitan tu permiso de notificaciones.

Puedes administrar los permisos desde Ajustes de iOS, restaurar compras y solicitar la eliminación de la cuenta desde la app cuando esta función esté disponible. Desinstalar la app no cancela automáticamente una suscripción. El servicio de respuestas revisado actualmente utiliza ejemplos locales; esta política deberá actualizarse si se habilita un servicio remoto de IA.

Importante: No compartas datos personales o confidenciales en solicitudes públicas de GitHub. La política debe verificarse frente a la configuración real de la versión publicada.
